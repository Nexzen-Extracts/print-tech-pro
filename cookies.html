What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. These files store certain information about your browsing activity and help websites function efficiently.

Cookies can be either session-based (deleted when you close your browser) or persistent (stored for a longer period). They help websites remember user preferences, improve performance, and provide insights into user behavior.
